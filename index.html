How it works

  1. Read the pixels

    Your PNG is drawn onto a canvas in this tab. Nothing is uploaded; there is no server.

  2. Split into colour layers

    A median-cut palette groups similar colours, then each colour becomes its own black-and-white mask.

  3. Trace every layer

    Potrace, compiled to WebAssembly, fits Bézier curves to each mask. The layers are stacked into one SVG you can inspect, edit and download.

Tracewell runs entirely in your browser — no uploads, no tracking.
